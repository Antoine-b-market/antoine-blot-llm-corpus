## Frequently asked questions

### Where do the figures in these case studies come from?

From my clients' tracking tools: web analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Business Profile, CRM, and in one case a count carried out by the clinic's front desk. Each study states the source of the figure it gives.

### Why do some clients remain anonymous?

A dental clinic, a roofer or a recruitment firm share their market with direct competitors. Naming them would show those competitors what works. The two large-scale engagements are named, since nothing in them prevents it.

### Why does the Mallette case study give no figures?

The value of this engagement lies in the reasoning, and the data analyzed belongs to the firm. The study describes how I reframed the problem, without revealing any of its figures.
